# BabySunRay — Store Owner's Guide

*A practical guide to running your shop, from the first product to the end of a working day.*

**Who this is for:** The store owner and anyone learning how the business works. No technical knowledge is needed.

**Website:** [babysunray.com](https://babysunray.com)  
**Admin:** [babysunray.com/admin](https://babysunray.com/admin)

> **Before public launch:** The website is currently private. Its working shop and checkout are available only for approved pre-launch checks. Do not enter real customer details or take real orders until the owner has completed launch preparation and confirmed the shop is open.

---

## Start here

BabySunRay helps you keep your products, stock, orders and cash-on-delivery records in one place.

There are two sides to the website:

| **Your Admin** | **The customer shop** |
|---|---|
| Add products and photos, record stock, review orders, manage deliveries and check reports. | Browse clothes, choose a size, add items to the bag and place an order. |
| Private. Only an authorized owner can enter. | Available to shoppers after the owner opens the store to the public. |
| Interface in English. Product details can be entered in English and Bangla. | Customer pages support English and বাংলা. |

### The everyday workflow

**Set up your store → Add a product → Add sizes and prices → Receive stock → Review and publish → Receive an order → Confirm → Pack → Dispatch → Record delivery → Record COD money → Review reports**

You do not need to complete all of this on the same day. Each part can be done when you are ready.

### A few words you will see often

- **Draft:** A product you are working on. Customers cannot buy it yet.
- **Variant / SKU:** One specific version of a product, such as *Cream Romper, 3–6 months*. A variant has its own price and stock. You may see a short stock code called an SKU.
- **Available:** Stock that is on hand and not already held for an order.
- **Published:** A product that is approved for the customer shop.
- **COD:** Cash on delivery. A delivery does not automatically mean the money has reached you.
- **RTO:** Return to origin — a parcel that comes back without being delivered.

1. Open [Admin sign-in](https://babysunray.com/admin/login).

3. After signing in, choose **Dashboard** to see what needs attention.

**At present, the application has an Owner account, not a full staff-account system.** Sharing this guide does not give someone permission to sign in. Never share the Owner password as a shortcut.

## 2. Set up your store

Open [Store setup](https://babysunray.com/admin/onboarding). It shows which essential details still need attention.

### Store name, description and contact details

1. Open **Store settings**.
2. Enter the name customers should see — for example, **BabySunRay**.
3. Add a short description in English and Bangla. Keep it factual and easy to understand.
4. Enter the phone number and email that customers should use.
5. Add a WhatsApp number or Messenger link **only if that channel is ready for customers**.
6. Choose **Save**.

You can return to this screen and edit your details later. Leaving an optional field empty is better than showing a contact method that nobody checks.

### Your logo

The **Logo** section lets you upload or replace the image shown for the shop. Use the approved business logo. A clear image with a transparent background is usually easiest to use on different backgrounds.

You can change the logo later; you do not need to settle on a final logo to learn the rest of Admin.

### Delivery charges

1. Open **Delivery charges**.
2. Find **Dhaka City** and **Outside Dhaka**.
3. Enter the actual charge you intend to collect for each area.
4. Activate the areas you can deliver to, then save.

The customer will see the delivery charge before placing the order. Do not promise delivery times or areas you cannot support.

### Categories

Open **Categories** and create useful groups such as *Bodysuits*, *Rompers* and *Sets*. Use names your customers will recognize. Add both English and Bangla names where requested.

**Business rule:** Do not publish delivery, refund or privacy promises until you have reviewed and approved the exact wording.

## 3. Add your first product

Open **Products → Add product**.

### Step A — Give the product a clear name

Start with the English name. Add the Bangla name in its own tab. The web address is suggested automatically, and you can change it under the advanced option when needed.

A good name tells a parent what the item is. For example:

- English: **Cotton Baby Romper**
- বাংলা: **কটন বেবি রম্পার**

Use the real product name. Avoid claims such as “100% organic,” “hypoallergenic” or “safest” unless you have evidence to support them.

### Step B — Describe what you're selling

Add the description in both languages. Mention only details you know: fabric, fit, available sizes, care instructions and anything the buyer should know.

If you enter fabric information, use the product's real label or confirmed supplier information. Follow any **Confirm** or **Reviewed** step shown on the page; typing information alone does not mean it has been approved.

### Step C — Add sizes, colours and prices

Open the product's variants section and choose **Add a variant**.

For each version, enter:

- **Size**, such as *0–3 months* or *3–6 months*
- **Colour**, if applicable
- **Price in taka**
- The unique SKU shown or requested by the form

Each size/colour combination is managed separately. If the same item comes in two sizes, add two variants so stock stays accurate.

For fitted clothing, enter the actual measurements in centimetres using the size/measurements section. This helps parents choose the right size.

### Step D — Add product photos

Open the product's **Photos** section. Upload clear photos of the actual item. A useful set is a front view, back view and fabric/detail view.

Add a short photo description in English and Bangla when requested. Keep the background tidy and the lighting consistent.

**Do not use another shop's photos without permission.** The first photo is particularly important because it is used in product lists.

### Step E — Save as a draft

New products start as **Draft**. This means you can keep editing without shoppers seeing an unfinished product.

**Need to add many products?** Use **Import products** from the Products page. Download the provided template, fill it in, review any errors in the preview, and import. An import creates drafts; it does not publish products or add physical stock.

## 4. Record stock properly

A product can have a price and photo but still be unavailable if you have not received stock.

### Receive new stock

1. Open the product and choose the correct size/colour variant.
2. Open its stock/SKU page.
3. Choose **Receive stock**.
4. Enter the number of pieces that arrived.
5. Confirm the entry and check the new total.

### Understand the stock numbers

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **On hand** | Physical units you have recorded |
| **Reserved** | Units being held for orders not yet dispatched |
| **Available** | On-hand units that are not reserved |
| **Not for sale** | A product or variant customers cannot currently buy, even if units remain physically on hand |

For example, with 10 pieces on hand and 3 held for orders, **7 are available**.

### Correct a mistake

Use the stock adjustment option and enter the real reason. Do not create a fake customer order to correct stock.

### Count your shelves

Use **Stock counts** to start a physical count, record what is actually there, review differences and apply the approved correction. It is safer to count regularly than to wait until a customer buys something you cannot find.

**Important:** Editing a product's price does not change its stock. Recording a dispatch or an approved return may change stock as part of the order process.

## 5. Review and publish a product

A complete product is not automatically public.

1. Open **Catalogue checklist** to see missing information.
2. Fix the items needed for publication.
3. Open the product or **Product reviews**.
4. Check the names, descriptions, sizes, prices and photos.
5. Mark the current content as **Reviewed**.
6. When your publication requirements are approved, choose **Publish to the shop** on the product page.

If you change important product details after review, you may need to review them again before publishing.

**Published** means the product is eligible to appear in the customer shop. It does not mean the entire website is publicly open.

Use **Withdraw** when a product should stop appearing in the shop. **Archive** is for a product you no longer actively manage or sell. An archived product can still have physical stock recorded.

## 6. Handle a new order

Open **Orders**. Search using an order number, customer name or full mobile number. Use the queue filters to find work that needs attention.

The usual flow is:

**Received → Confirmed → Packed → Dispatched → Delivered**

The order page also keeps payment and return information separate. Do not assume that changing the delivery status also records cash received.

### Confirm the order

1. Open the order, or choose the available **Confirm/Verify** action.
2. Contact the customer through your agreed method.
3. Check the name, phone number, address, products and delivery area.
4. Confirm only after the customer agrees.

The normal verification window is 24 hours. **You can still confirm an overdue order.** A warning appears, and the system checks whether the stock is still available. If it isn't, resolve the shortage rather than promising an item you don't have.

### Pack and dispatch

1. Use **Mark packed** after checking the physical items.
2. Choose **Dispatch** when the parcel actually leaves your shop.
3. Record **Delivery** after you have reliable confirmation that it arrived.

**Record delivery is your business record, not automatic proof from a courier.** There is no automatic courier delivery confirmation connected to this step.

On a computer you can select multiple eligible orders for some actions, including packing and dispatch. Always review the selection and the individual results. Some orders may succeed while others need attention.

### Need to cancel?

Open the order and follow the available cancellation action. Check whether it has already been packed or dispatched; later stages may need a different handling process. Never erase an order just because it was cancelled.

### Taking an order over chat or by phone

Use **New assisted order** if a customer places an order through an agreed channel such as WhatsApp or Messenger. Enter the correct customer details, items and delivery area, then review the total before saving.

Only create a real order when the customer has actually ordered. Do not create an order merely to make a dashboard number change.

## 7. Understand cash on delivery

Cash on delivery has several steps. This distinction protects your business.

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Order placed** | The customer has requested items; this is not cash received |
| **Delivered** | The parcel was recorded as delivered |
| **Collected by courier** | The courier says it collected money from the customer |
| **Settled to you** | The money was actually paid to your business |
| **Courier deduction** | An amount kept by the courier, recorded separately |

Example: a courier collects **৳ 1,000**, keeps **৳ 80** as an agreed charge and sends **৳ 920** to you. Record each figure in the appropriate place instead of calling the full ৳ 1,000 your received money.

Open an order's **Cash on delivery** section to record collections, settlements and deductions. Record only amounts supported by your courier statement or actual payment evidence.

Use **Reports → COD & reconciliation** to find money still to collect or settle.

**BabySunRay does not currently take an online card or mobile-wallet payment through checkout.** Customer orders use COD.

## 8. Returns, undelivered parcels and refunds

If a customer requests a return or exchange, open the order's return section. Follow the conditions in the store policy you have approved.

A parcel that comes back undelivered is called **RTO**. Record the return and any relevant charge separately from a successful delivery.

When physical items come back:

1. Record their arrival.
2. Inspect them before making them available to sell again.
3. Restock only items that pass inspection.
4. Record an approved refund only when the amount is actually owed and supported by the original payment record.

Do not put damaged or uninspected items straight back into available stock.

An exchange may require a separate replacement order. Check the charges and stock before promising a replacement.

## 9. Reports and daily checks

Open **Reports** and choose a date range: Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month or a custom period.

The reports group information into:

- **Orders & performance:** How many orders were placed and their current status
- **COD & reconciliation:** Money collected, money settled, courier deductions and money still outstanding
- **Inventory & costs:** Stock movements and recorded costs

Remember: **Order value is not the same as revenue received, and money collected by a courier is not the same as money settled to you.**

Use **Printable reconciliation** when you need a paper record. Use **Exports** to download business information for recordkeeping or review.

**Privacy tip:** Some exports include customer names, phone numbers and addresses. Keep those files private, share only with people who need them, and delete unnecessary copies.

### A simple end-of-day routine

- Check **Needs attention** and overdue orders.
- Confirm new orders and pack the ones ready to leave.
- Follow up dispatched parcels and record confirmed deliveries.
- Reconcile COD against actual courier information.
- Review low stock and products that need replenishing.
- Sign out when you're finished.

## 10. Policies customers can read

Open **Policies** to prepare the store's wording in English and Bangla.

The areas include delivery, returns/exchanges, privacy and checkout notices.

The usual process is:

**Write → Save draft → Review the exact wording → Approve and publish**

A saved draft is not necessarily visible to customers. Publishing requires the owner's approval.

Write only what your business can actually honor. If a delivery charge, return condition or data-use explanation changes, review the customer-facing policy again.

For legal/privacy wording, get qualified advice where appropriate. The Admin does not automatically confirm that a policy complies with every law.

## 11. How customers use the shop

When the owner has officially opened the shop, shoppers will be able to:

1. Open the website and choose English or বাংলা.
2. Browse categories or search for a product.
3. Open a product and check its photos, size information and price.
4. Choose an available size and colour.
5. Add it to the **Bag**.
6. Enter their name, mobile number, delivery address and delivery area.
7. Review the COD total and choose **Place order**.
8. Keep the order number and private tracking link from the confirmation.

Customers can place a **guest order**, without creating an account. An account is available for customers who prefer email/password or Google sign-in, when those options are enabled.

Placing an order means it was **received**, not that the store has confirmed it. The Admin still needs to verify it.

### Order tracking

The tracking link is private. Customers should save it and not post it publicly. The tracking page shows the order's progress and the customer actions allowed at that stage.

A missing tracking link should be handled through your customer support channel; the owner can create a new one from the order page when appropriate.

### Before the public opening

The real shop and checkout are restricted during pre-launch preparation. **Do not tell customers to place orders yet.** The owner should finish the shop acceptance and launch checks first.

## 12. Test the complete shopping journey before launch

The **Shop acceptance** page lets the owner check the real store flow privately.

Use fictional customer details and a sample product, not a real stranger's personal information.

The simple checklist is:

- [ ] Enter the store name and delivery charges.
- [ ] Create a product with a photo, sizes and prices.
- [ ] Receive stock and review the product.
- [ ] Publish it in the private acceptance shop.
- [ ] Visit the customer shop in a separate private browser window.
- [ ] Add an item to the bag and place a COD order.
- [ ] Find the same order in Admin.
- [ ] Confirm, pack, dispatch and record delivery.
- [ ] Record COD collection and settlement.
- [ ] Check stock, tracking and reports.
- [ ] Try a cancellation and an out-of-stock purchase.
- [ ] End the acceptance session and review **Launch readiness**.

This is a private rehearsal. **Do not use the site's “Make public” button to start it.** Opening the store publicly is a separate owner decision.

## 13. Account safety and handling customer details

- Use a unique, strong Owner password.
- Keep your recovery codes somewhere safe and separate from your daily browsing.
- Sign out of Admin on shared computers.
- Do not share order screenshots with a customer's full phone number or address visible.
- Avoid giving someone your Owner login. The current system does not offer general staff invitations.
- Treat tracking links as private customer information.
- Record deliveries and COD only when you have real confirmation.

If you suspect someone has access to your Owner account, change your password, review account security and ask a trusted administrator for help before continuing sensitive work.

## 14. When something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Can't sign in** | Check the email and password; use recovery if necessary. |
| **An action says the record changed** | Another tab or person may have updated it. Reload, review the latest details and try again. |
| **Product doesn't appear in the shop** | Check whether it's still a draft, missing review, withdrawn, archived or unavailable. Also confirm the shop is open. |
| **Can't publish a product** | Open **Catalogue checklist** and **Publication requirements**. Complete the missing items and current review. |
| **Stock appears wrong** | Check the correct variant, the stock history and any reserved orders. Use **Stock counts** if you need to reconcile physical units. |
| **Can't confirm an overdue order** | Check the stock warning. Late confirmation is allowed only if the stock can still be safely reserved. |
| **Customer didn't receive a signup email** | Check the address and spam folder; use the site's resend or reset option where offered. |
| **Customer can't sign in with Google** | Use guest checkout if available, or contact the store. The owner should request technical help to check sign-in configuration. |
| **Money doesn't match your courier statement** | Compare the order-level collection, deduction and settlement entries. Don't overwrite the numbers to make a report look right. |
| **A photo won't upload** | Try a clear JPG, PNG or WebP image, keep the file reasonably small, and try again. |
| **A page says private or access denied** | You may be signed out, or the website may still be in pre-launch protection. Don't change its public-access settings to work around the error. |

### Need help?

Write down the page, what you clicked, what you expected and the message shown. A screenshot is useful **only after hiding passwords, recovery codes and customer information**.

## 15. Features not included in everyday operation

To avoid confusion, these are **not** currently automatic:

- Courier booking or courier tracking integration
- Online card/mobile-wallet payment collection
- A general staff invitation and role-management system
- Automatic confirmation of a delivery by the courier
- Automatic transfer of courier-collected COD into your bank account

You can still run an ordinary COD business using the tools described above, with the appropriate manual records.
