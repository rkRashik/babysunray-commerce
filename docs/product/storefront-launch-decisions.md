# Storefront launch decisions

**Status:** Draft — Founder Review
**Purpose:** Every decision still needed before the public storefront and checkout can open, in one place: what is already approved, what engineering recommends, and what is still open. Recommendations are not decisions and nothing here has been implemented as policy.

Sources: [requirements](requirements.md) (IDs below), [order policy decisions](order-policy-decisions.md) (rules A to N), [storefront concepts](../design/storefront-concepts.md), [launch readiness](../operations/launch-readiness.md). Public checkout stays disabled until S-17 and G-7 are met; the launch gate refuses whatever is decided here until a later gate builds the public pages.

| Topic | Approved | Recommended default (trade-off) | Still open |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Design direction** | The logo, its silhouette and its three colours as a brand reference (BR-1 to BR-3); premium, restrained, editorial, warm, not a template, equal quality on a phone (S-2). | Concept C, the hybrid: A's typography and photography with B's search, chips and phone buying bar, on the token set in storefront-concepts section 2. *Trade-off:* more editorial than B, so slightly more scrolling on a phone than a dense shop. | Final palette, typography (a brand typeface would need a licence and a download), layout; whether to test A, B and C with people first (G-9). |
| **Product publication** | Bilingual name and description (S-1); an active SKU with a price (I-9, I-10); no invented claims and a fabric statement only after confirmation (S-11). | Publishable when every product item of the readiness checklist is met (name, description, fabric statement and care in both languages, the fabric statement confirmed; measurements in cm for every size on sale; every active SKU sized; a category; at least one photo with alt text in both languages) **and** the product has a current internal review. Sold-out products stay visible, marked sold out. *Trade-off:* a stricter rule slows how fast the catalogue grows; it is the rule I-6 proposes. | Approval of I-6 itself; visibility of sold-out products; whether unpublishing is needed; who may publish when staff exist (OF-7). |
| **Product photos** | The Owner's own photos only, never a competitor's (S-11); uploads are checked, re-encoded and stripped of metadata (built). | At least one photo, ideally three (front, back, a fabric detail), portrait 4:5 to match the tiles, at least 1,600 pixels on the long side, a consistent plain background, alt text in both languages. *Trade-off:* more photos per product take more shooting time. | How many photos a product needs (I-5); production photo storage and its backup (decision 14, depends on hosting). |
| **Checkout wording** | Submission is received, not confirmed, until the Owner verifies it (rule B, S-8); the delivery charge is shown before submission (S-7, rule I); cash on delivery (S-4). | Approve the ten draft statements in storefront-concepts section 3 as written, or edit them. Replace the phone hint's example number with a clearly fictional one. *Trade-off:* none; this only fixes words. | The final words in both languages; which contact route (phone, Messenger, WhatsApp) the checkout and error messages show (S-12). |
| **Search and customer accounts** | Guest checkout with no forced account (S-3); no paid SMS dependency (S-9). | Launch with product search and filters (built in the preview) and guest checkout only; no accounts at launch. Revisit Google sign-in after launch if customers ask for order history. *Trade-off:* repeat customers type their details each time and keep their own tracking links. | Launch scope of search, accounts, wishlists and reviews (S-14). Options compared in storefront-concepts section 4. |
| **Delivery zones and fees** | Two zones, Dhaka City and Outside Dhaka, charges set by the Owner and stored with each order; no order without a configured charge (rule I, O-8). | Publish which areas count as Dhaka City as a short list the customer reads before choosing; keep the charge visible on the product page, in the cart and at checkout (built). No amount is proposed. *Trade-off:* a list is simpler than district rules but needs care at the boundary. | The amounts; the definition of Dhaka City; any district-level zones (O-8); delivery times (none may be promised until decided). |
| **Published policies** | Delivery, return and exchange, and privacy policies must be founder-approved **and published** before public checkout; placeholder text is not acceptable (S-17, G-7). The order rules they rest on are approved (rules A to N). | Finish the four drafts in the Office (policy drafts), have the privacy policy checked by a qualified person, then approve; a later gate builds the public policy pages and links them from the footer and checkout. *Trade-off:* legal review takes time but is the only safe basis for privacy text. | The wording; legal review; terms of sale (if needed); how customers ask to see, correct or delete their data; the publication mechanism (not built). |
| **Guest checkout and tracking** | No account; a private random tracking link per order, never a lookup by order number and phone (rule K, S-9); the customer may cancel while Pending Verification (rule C); 24-hour verification window (rule D). The service is built and tested but not reachable (ADR-0007). | Show the tracking link once on the confirmation with a copy button and the note already drafted; the Owner also sends it during the verification call or message, which covers a lost confirmation. Abuse protection (per-client limits) before opening. *Trade-off:* sending the link by hand costs the Owner a step per order, but needs no paid SMS. | How the link reaches customers who lose it (Owner by hand is the default); abuse protection, which needs the hosting decision; whether SMS or WhatsApp delivery of links is ever paid for (C-1). |

Also needed before launch but outside the storefront's own design: production hosting (decision 1), recovery objectives and backups (decisions 6 and 15), account recovery for the admin (OF-5) and a usability test with real people (G-9). The current state of each is on the admin panel's Launch readiness page.
